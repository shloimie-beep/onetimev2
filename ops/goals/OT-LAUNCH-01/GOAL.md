# OT-LAUNCH-01

Deliver one production-believable, persistent One Time staging application where an Administrator can operate or safely preview the complete system without changing production.

GHL owns adult enrollment and communication. Current status lives only in [BOARD.yaml](BOARD.yaml). Requirements live in [SPEC.yaml](SPEC.yaml), acceptance in [ACCEPTANCE.yaml](ACCEPTANCE.yaml), and durable choices in [DECISIONS.yaml](DECISIONS.yaml).
