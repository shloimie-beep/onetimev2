# OT-LAUNCH-01

Deliver one governed One Time system that progresses from persistent staging into a controlled live production pilot where the operator can use the real Administrator, Parent, Student, Classroom, content, Rabbi, and provider experiences without broad customer rollout.

GHL owns adult enrollment, customer communication, payment operations, and payment history. One Time owns authentication, the resulting account/product access state, Parent and separate Student identities, classrooms, protected Zoom launch, content, Vimeo delivery, progress, questions, the Rabbi console, and provider-truthful unavailable states. A free pilot may grant access without fabricating a GHL payment. All ordinary staging role experiences use one coherent fictional Cohen household instead of random fixtures.

GitHub is the canonical desired-state and control view for HighLevel. Browser work may apply only a reviewed Git-authored job, and every change closes through save, reopen/readback, a bounded canary when authorized, sanitized committed evidence, and drift validation. UI intent likewise closes through breakpoint-specific acceptance, current-page/screenshot inspection, obvious in-scope repair, retest, and deployed-URL verification.

The operator sees a concise Board-derived launch summary: what just changed, what works now, the active milestone percentage, exact blockers, safe links, and the next executable task. This surface is a generated projection and never a second editable status model.

Current status lives only in [BOARD.yaml](BOARD.yaml). Requirements live in [SPEC.yaml](SPEC.yaml), acceptance in [ACCEPTANCE.yaml](ACCEPTANCE.yaml), and durable choices in [DECISIONS.yaml](DECISIONS.yaml).
