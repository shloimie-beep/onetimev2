## 1. How to read this register

This register resolves product choices before roadmap generation.

- **LOCKED** means the operator explicitly decided the matter.
- **INFERRED** means the operator asked the specification author to use best judgment. An inferred decision is normative for v2.1 unless the operator later changes it.
- **DEFERRED** means the capability is not part of launch and must be absent or safely disabled.
- **HISTORICAL** means evidence may be retained, but it may not drive current product behavior or release status.

If another file conflicts with this register, the v2.1 package wins for product behavior. Security, privacy, child-safety, financial-integrity, data-isolation, migration-integrity, and provider-idempotency safeguards may not be weakened silently; any such conflict requires a recorded replacement decision and acceptance proof.

## 16. Explicitly resolved ambiguities

1. **Parent learner access:** No Parent Learner Mode. The adult may consume one Student seat with separate credentials.
2. **School support:** School public entry is sales-led. Approved schools use the same product model; no school portal or role is created.
3. **WhatsApp launch:** Email works independently. WhatsApp is dormant until configured and approved.
4. **Multiple guardians:** One account owner per household; verified ownership transfer replaces co-guardian access.
5. **Multiple households:** One adult identity may own several independent household subscriptions.
6. **Testing:** Production test/demo surfaces are removed; safety verification remains.
7. **Inactive access:** Parent can reach only the exact overview/switcher/billing/support/account/privacy/data-rights allowlist; Students cannot enter learning surfaces.
8. **Student multi-device classroom:** One concurrent session, with same-session reconnect.
9. **Campaign authoring:** GHL is the campaign authoring/execution surface.
10. **Content ingestion:** Direct app upload and Drive monitoring are two entrances to one pipeline.
