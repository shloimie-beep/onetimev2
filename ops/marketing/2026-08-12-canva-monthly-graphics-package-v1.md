## 3. Brand kit for Canva

### Core colors

- Near-black: `#050505`
- One Time yellow: `#FFD21F`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Ice/cyan accent: `#059ED1` — use sparingly for tiny labels only

### Type system

Use only two primary font families in the Canva package:

- **DM Serif Display** — emotional/editorial headline, hero statement, Rabbi/authority pieces
- **Montserrat** — all-caps labels, dates, subheads, buttons, small utility copy

Recommended Canva weights:

- DM Serif Display Regular
- Montserrat ExtraBold / Bold / SemiBold

### Typography rule

- One main message per graphic.
- Main headline: 3–9 words whenever possible.
- Highlight one phrase in yellow, not the entire headline.
- Use cyan only for a small eyebrow such as `LIVE FROM ERETZ YISRAEL`.
- No paragraphs baked into normal organic graphics.
- Avoid outlines, glow, fake 3D type, multiple shadows, and random fonts.

## 10. Canva layout rules

- Maintain at least 7–9% outer safe margin.
- Keep faces clear of copy.
- Never cover Rabbi's eyes/mouth/hands when the gesture matters.
- Avoid putting copy across Mishnah text when the text is being used as proof.
- Use black gradient instead of black translucent rounded boxes whenever possible.
- Use a yellow button/pill only when the graphic actually has a CTA.
- No more than one CTA per graphic.
- Keep logos small; do not make logo the focal point.
- Use real photography far more often than decorative illustrations.
