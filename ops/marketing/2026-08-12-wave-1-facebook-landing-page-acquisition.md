# One Time Mishnayos — Wave 1 Acquisition Path

**Decision date:** 2026-08-12
**Owner:** Shloimie Dratler
**Status:** Current marketing execution priority

## Decision

Wave 1 acquisition is **not a WhatsApp funnel**.

The acquisition path is:

1. **Facebook ads / organic Facebook creative**
2. **Eligible connected Instagram placements through Meta when useful**
3. **One destination: the One Time landing/signup page**
4. **Family registration / immediate free access**

YouTube is a later acquisition wave after the Meta creative and landing-page conversion loop is operating.

WhatsApp is not the lead destination, not the CTA destination, and not part of the conversion journey. Do not design or optimize this campaign around click-to-WhatsApp, WhatsApp lead capture, a WhatsApp assistant, or WhatsApp API messaging.

If an operator manually shares a landing-page link in WhatsApp, that is incidental distribution only and does not change the funnel architecture or creative priorities.

## Funnel

```text
Facebook / Instagram creative
        ↓
join.onetimeonetime.com
        ↓
GET FREE ACCESS
        ↓
Create Family account
        ↓
Immediate free-access product journey
```

## Creative implications

- Design primarily for Meta feed and vertical placements.
- Keep baked-in text minimal so the same visual can support multiple Meta ad-copy tests.
- Normal ad creative: one short hook or headline on-image, usually 2–7 words.
- Put offer explanation, schedule, dates, supporting persuasion, and landing-page link in Meta Primary Text / Headline / CTA fields rather than cramming them into the image.
- Date/urgency graphics may include one short second line when the date is itself the message.
- Build 1080×1350 feed masters first.
- Build 1080×1920 Story/Reel adaptations from approved winners.
- Do not create a separate Instagram-first visual system.
- Do not create WhatsApp-specific Canva masters for Wave 1.

## Current launch CTA

Primary CTA: `GET FREE ACCESS`.

The Family path is registration/account creation with immediate free access, not `Pre-register`.

- Free access ends September 11, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. Jerusalem time.
- No card is required for the free-access period.

## Guardrails

- Do not use `Pre-register` in current acquisition creative after the operator's 2026-08-12 decision.
- Do not claim `the biggest Mishnayos class in the world` as an established fact without evidence; aspirational wording such as `Help us build the biggest Mishnayos class in the world` is acceptable.
- No WhatsApp lead assistant or click-to-WhatsApp acquisition path.
