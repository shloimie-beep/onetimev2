# One Time Mishnayos — Wave 1 Channel Priority

**Decision date:** 2026-08-12
**Owner:** Shloimie Dratler
**Status:** Current marketing execution priority

## Decision

Wave 1 launch marketing is:

1. **WhatsApp** — direct/broadcast/share distribution to approved adult audiences.
2. **Facebook** — organic Page activity plus paid Facebook ads driving to the One Time landing/signup flow.
3. **Instagram** — secondary Meta placement/distribution when the connected professional Instagram account and campaign settings allow it; do not create a separate Instagram-first creative program yet.
4. **YouTube** — later wave after Facebook/WhatsApp launch creative is operating.

## Creative implications

- Canva masters must be reusable across Facebook and WhatsApp.
- Keep baked-in text minimal so the same visual can be reused with different Facebook Primary Text and WhatsApp message copy.
- Normal ad creative: one short 2–7 word hook on-image; supporting offer, dates, explanation, and URL belong in the post/message copy.
- Date/urgency graphics may include one short second line when the date is the point of the creative.
- Build 1080×1350 feed masters first.
- Build 1080×1920 adaptations for WhatsApp Status / Meta Stories/Reels where needed.
- Do not create a separate Instagram visual system; use Meta placement adaptations from the Facebook masters.
- Keep one clear destination: current live One Time Family signup/free-access flow.

## WhatsApp execution note

Do not conflate:

- WhatsApp Status / manual sharing;
- WhatsApp Business app broadcast lists; and
- paid/API marketing template sends.

Before any paid/API broadcast, verify the exact audience, permission/opt-in basis, sending method, price shown in the live provider UI, and applicable message/template requirements. Do not assume a fixed send cost from memory.

## Facebook execution note

Use Meta Ads Manager. Facebook is the primary named paid surface, but eligible connected Instagram placements may be included by Meta campaign placement settings. Keep placement-specific previews enabled and exclude placements only when the creative renders badly or the campaign goal requires it.

## Current CTA

Use the current launch decision: `GET FREE ACCESS` / Family registration, not `Pre-register`.
