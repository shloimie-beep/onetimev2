## 3. CapCut and Natural-Language Editing Decision

### Recommendation

Use a **hybrid workflow**:

1. **Codex prepares the media intelligence:** inventory, dedupe, transcripts, exact timecodes, clean clips, rights lane, energy scores, and build sheet.
2. **CapCut Desktop creates the rough cut:** use Auto Cut, beat sync, speech-pause detection, or AI Script Mode when those features are present in the installed version.
3. **A locked CapCut template controls the final design:** fonts, black/yellow palette, safe zones, three text-motion presets, two transition families, captions, Rabbi voice windows, and CTA.
4. **Shloimie reviews manually:** identity, rights, copy, pacing, sound, and CTA.
5. **Upload manually:** publishing automation is out of scope.

Natural language is useful for the first assembly. It is not reliable enough to control exact source selection, rights status, face integrity, repeatable kinetic typography, or final brand consistency by itself.

### Controlled motion system

Use three reusable text motions rather than random effects:

- **OT-SWEEP:** a yellow bar or mask sweeps horizontally; the phrase follows across the frame.
- **OT-PUNCH:** one to three words scale quickly from approximately 92% to 105% and settle at 100%.
- **OT-WORD-STACK:** words enter in a staggered sequence, then clear before the next visual beat.

Use primarily hard cuts and punch-ins. Keep only two transition families:

- fast directional wipe between major sections;
- short blur/zoom transition only where the soundtrack calls for it.

Do not place a transition on every clip. The footage should remain the main event.

## 7. Template V1 — 30-Second Vertical Master

**Canvas:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps  
**Target duration:** 28–31 seconds  
**Style:** fast, real, energetic, clear, black/yellow with restrained ice/cyan  
**Audio:** music-led, with two short Rabbi voice breaks  
**Captions:** required whenever Rabbi voice is audible

| Time | Visual and audio | Text motion |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–1.2 | Strongest Rabbi expression, gesture, or immediate student reaction | Opening hook using OT-PUNCH or OT-WORD-STACK |
| 1.2–4.2 | Three or four fast teaching/class cuts | No additional text unless essential |
| 4.2–5.4 | Full-screen or footage-backed transition beat | OT-SWEEP: `A REAL REBBE` or the selected proof phrase |
| 5.4–9.0 | Rabbi voice bite; music ducks | Captions only |
| 9.0–13.5 | Student engagement and wide class proof | One short overlay maximum |
| 13.5–14.8 | Major text beat | OT-PUNCH: `ONE PEREK AT A TIME` |
| 14.8–19.5 | Board/device, sefer, slides, and Rabbi pointing | `LIVE + ON DEMAND` may appear once |
| 19.5–22.5 | Second Rabbi voice bite | Captions only |
| 22.5–26.8 | Fast payoff montage: Rabbi, students, screen, accomplishment | No clutter |
| 26.8–30.0 | Hero Rabbi image or footage over clean black/yellow finish | CTA verified against live page; safe default `PRE-REGISTER YOUR FAMILY` |

### Pacing rules

- Most shots: 0.6–1.8 seconds.
- Voice shots may remain 2–5 seconds.
- Use visual continuity: gesture direction, gaze, pointing, board position, or beat.
- Text should appear on three or four meaningful beats, not on every cut.
- Keep faces, mouths, sefarim, and key teaching content unobstructed.
- Keep essential text inside platform-safe margins.
